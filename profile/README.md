# Altru Robotics

**[altrurobotics.com](https://altrurobotics.com)** · Chennai, India · A DPIIT-recognised startup

Altru Robotics designs and builds robots and business software in Chennai, India.

## Products

- **[tBot](https://altrurobotics.com/products/tbot)**: an autonomous indoor guidance robot. It learns a building once. Then it greets visitors, answers questions and walks them where they need to go.
- **[Integer](https://altrurobotics.com/products/integer)**: a business suite for manufacturing companies. HR and payroll, sales, production, purchase, quality and accounts in one system. You buy it once and run it on your own server.

## Engineering

Our robot platform is **AptOS**: robot runtime and control, Android operator apps, embedded tooling for Arduino and ESP32, and ROS 2 integration for mapping, navigation and sensing.

Engineering repositories are private by default. Archived repositories stay for history.
